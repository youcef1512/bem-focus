BEM Focus Offline

English: reading and vocabulary moves

اللغة الإنجليزية

How to find the topic, catch the main idea, and use the text vocabulary without getting lost.

30 min
You should answer short reading questions with confidence and clean English.

قبل ما تبدأي

Read the title and write one topic word before reading the whole text.

Write one sentence with school or environment.

الأهداف

  • Find the topic quickly
  • Answer from the text without copying blindly
  • Use context to infer meaning

1) Title and first line

The title and first sentence usually reveal the topic.

Do not start translating every word. Start by finding the general meaning.

2) Main idea

The main idea is bigger than one detail.

Ask: what is this text mainly trying to say?

3) Vocabulary in context

A word can be understood from the sentence around it.

Look at the idea before and after the word, not the word alone.
