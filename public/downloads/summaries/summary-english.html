BEM Focus Summary

English Quick Sheet: full programme

اللغة الإنجليزية

الخلاصة

  • Read the title and first line before answering.
  • Use short sentences with clear subject + verb + complement.
  • Revise connectors, tenses, modals, and paragraph structure.

البرنامج الكامل في المادة

Reading

  • Title and topic
  • Main idea
  • Specific information
  • True/false with justification
  • Understanding from context

Vocabulary

  • Word meaning in context
  • Synonym / opposite when required
  • Topic vocabulary
  • Useful classroom and social themes

Grammar

  • Sentence order
  • Connectors
  • Question forms
  • Negation
  • Pronouns and basic references

Tenses and modals

  • Present simple
  • Past simple basics
  • Future with will / going to when required
  • Can / should / must
  • Time markers

Writing

  • Short paragraph
  • Giving advice
  • Expressing opinion
  • Describing habits or events
  • Closing sentence

ترتيب المراجعة

  • Reading and topic detection
  • Vocabulary in context
  • Grammar and sentence building
  • Tenses and modals
  • Paragraph writing and advice/opinion

Memory hooks

  • One idea per sentence.
  • Capitals and punctuation matter in writing.

حركات امتحانية

  • Underline the keyword in the question.
  • Reuse correct vocabulary from the text when useful.
  • Build a paragraph from topic sentence + support + ending.

أخطاء شائعة

  • Translating every word and losing the global meaning.
  • Using long broken sentences instead of short correct ones.
  • Writing without a clear topic sentence.

فحص سريع

  • I can find the main idea quickly.
  • I can write a correct sentence with should or because.
  • I can produce a short coherent paragraph.
